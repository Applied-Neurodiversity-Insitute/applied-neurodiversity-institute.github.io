# Applied Neurodiversity Institute

If we are to successfully navigate the next few decades, we need as many perspectives as possible to bring to bear on the tangled, overlapping problems we face. The Applied Neurodiversity Institute exists to be a part of that effort, particularly where those perspectives belong to neurodivergent people.

This is a new project being run by [Matthew Bellringer](https://www.matthewbellringer.com).

## Long-term goals

The intention of the project is to provide research, education, consultancy and funding on the issues relating to neurodiversity and the Everything Crisis (also called the [polycrisis](https://www.weforum.org/stories/health-and-healthcare-systems/polycrisis-adam-tooze-historian-explains/) or the metacrisis).

## Current

The project is in its very early stages at the moment. In the next few months we will start regular peer-support sessions for people working in this area. If you are interested in attending these, and/or in joining a private group chat with other practitioners, please email **info@appliedneurodiversity.org**.
